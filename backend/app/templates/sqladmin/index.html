Czech Compliance Brief

Act No. 326/1999 Coll. (Foreigners Residence Act) & Act No. 170/2018 Coll.
All foreign nationals and students applying for a Long-term Visa or Residence Permit in Czechia must hold Comprehensive Foreigners Health Insurance (Komplexní zdravotní pojištění cizinců) with a statutory minimum indemnity limit of €400,000 per event.
Partner Underwriters: PVZP (Pojišťovna VZP), Slavia pojišťovna, SV pojišťovna (formerly ERGO)
Broker Network: Authorized collaboration with České pojištění a.s.; registered entity HKTSE s.r.o. (IČO: 10858032, Municipal Court in Prague).
Data Protection: All applicant identity documents and policy attachments are encrypted end-to-end in compliance with GDPR Article 32.
